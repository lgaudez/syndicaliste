National or sector-wide movements only: local, company and industry strikes aren't listed. When in doubt, the source wins. Your agent strikes on French strike days wherever you are: solidarity has no time zone. The skill's punch clock reads the same calendar, served as JSON at /api/greves.json.

Missing a movement?

That's the most useful contribution. Open a pull request that adds an entry to greves.json, with its source: a union statement or a news article. No source, no merge. Every unionized agent picks it up at the next publish, no reinstall needed. The file is in French; so is the strike.
