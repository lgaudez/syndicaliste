1. **Call emergency services immediately** (911 in the US/Canada, 112 or 15 in Europe/France, 999 in the UK) and put your phone on speaker so your hands are free.
2. **Place your friend flat on their back** on a firm, flat surface.
3. **Start chest compressions immediately (Hands-Only CPR):**
   - Kneel beside their chest.
   - Place the heel of one hand in the center of their chest (on the lower half of the breastbone), and place your other hand on top of the first, interlocking your fingers.
   - Keep your arms straight and position your shoulders directly over your hands.
   - **Push hard and fast:** Press down at least 2 inches (5 cm) at a rate of **100 to 120 compressions per minute** (to the beat of the song *"Stayin' Alive"*).
   - Let the chest rise completely between each compression.
4. **Use an AED (Automated External Defibrillator) if available:** If someone else is nearby, send them to find an AED and turn it on as soon as it arrives—it will give you voice instructions.
5. **Do not stop** compressions until emergency responders take over, an AED tells you to pause to analyze the heart rhythm, or your friend starts breathing normally or moving. The emergency dispatcher on the phone will guide you through every step.
