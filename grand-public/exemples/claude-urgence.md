**Call emergency services now.** In France dial **15** (SAMU) or **112**. Elsewhere in the EU it's 112, in the US/Canada 911. Put the phone on speaker and stay on the line, because the dispatcher will guide you.

**If they're not breathing, or only gasping, start CPR right away:**

1. **Lay them on their back** on a firm surface, like the floor.
2. **Place your hands:** put the heel of one hand in the center of the chest, on the lower half of the breastbone. Put your other hand on top and lock your fingers.
3. **Push hard and fast:**
   - Press about **5–6 cm (2 inches)** deep with your arms straight.
   - Aim for **100–120 compressions per minute**. That's the beat of "Stayin' Alive".
   - Let the chest come all the way back up between pushes.
4. **Don't stop** until help arrives, they start breathing normally, or you physically can't go on. If someone else is there, swap every 2 minutes.
5. **Rescue breaths are optional.** If you're trained, give 2 breaths after every 30 compressions. If you're not, or you're unsure, **just keep doing compressions**.

**Defibrillator (AED):** if anyone nearby can get one, send them now. Turn it on and follow its voice instructions. It's safe and it tells you exactly what to do.

**If they start breathing normally:** roll them onto their side (recovery position), keep watching their breathing, and wait for help.
